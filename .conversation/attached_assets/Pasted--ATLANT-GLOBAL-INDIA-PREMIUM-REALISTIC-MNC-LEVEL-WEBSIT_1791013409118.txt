# ATLANT GLOBAL INDIA — PREMIUM, REALISTIC & MNC-LEVEL WEBSITE

Design and build a completely new website/web application for:

**ATLANT GLOBAL INDIA (INC)**

Operating under:

**THE PROFESSIONAL COURIERS**

Existing website:

https://www.atlantglobalindia.in/

The existing website must be used as the primary source for the company's actual:

* History
* Services
* Infrastructure
* Branches
* Statistics
* Leadership
* Testimonials
* FAQs
* Franchise information
* Contact information
* Business capabilities

The new website must preserve the real company information but completely redesign the user experience and visual presentation.

---

# CORE DESIGN DIRECTION

The website must look like a:

**PREMIUM INTERNATIONAL LOGISTICS COMPANY**

with the professionalism and credibility expected from an MNC.

However, the design must remain:

**REALISTIC**

**SIMPLE**

**ATTRACTIVE**

**CLEAN**

**PROFESSIONAL**

**TRUSTWORTHY**

**MODERN**

Do NOT make it look futuristic or like a technology demo.

Do NOT use excessive animation.

Do NOT use complicated 3D graphics.

Do NOT use artificial-looking animated logistics scenes.

Do NOT make the website look like a gaming interface.

Do NOT overload the page with visual effects.

The website should feel like a real company with real people, real vehicles, real branches, real warehouses and real logistics operations.

---

# VISUAL INSPIRATION

The overall visual quality should feel comparable to a major international logistics/corporate company.

Take inspiration from the professionalism of companies such as:

* DHL
* FedEx
* UPS
* Maersk
* DB Schenker
* Kuehne+Nagel

But DO NOT copy their branding, layouts, colours, logos or designs.

The goal is:

**MNC QUALITY + ATLANT GLOBAL INDIA IDENTITY**

# ANIMATION RULE

Animation must be MINIMAL.

Use only subtle UI interactions such as:

* Button hover
* Card hover
* Very subtle fade-in
* Smooth page transitions
* Simple accordion
* Image hover
* Small number counter animation if appropriate

No large animated graphics.

No continuously moving backgrounds.

No excessive scrolling effects.

No animated vehicles.

No animated maps.

No animated shipment routes.

The website must still look excellent if ALL animations are disabled.

This is extremely important.
